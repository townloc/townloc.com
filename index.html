FAQ

Questions we hear before getting started

Straight answers. If your question is about one service only, the page for that service has more.

What services do you provide?+

Six core services: Google Business Profile, Remove Negative Reviews, Website Building, Local SEO, Google Ads, and Google Reviews. Most local businesses need more than one over time.

Do you work with my type of business?+

Yes — if customers search Google before they call, book, or visit. We work with home services, trades, clinics, restaurants, retail, and other local businesses. See all industries.

Can you help if I don't have a Google Business Profile?+

Yes. We can claim, verify, and build out a new profile — or fix an existing one that is thin, wrong, or unclaimed. Start with Google Business Profile setup.

Can you help with negative or unfair Google reviews?+

Yes. We can assess the review, help you respond professionally, and guide you through appropriate reporting options when a review may violate Google's policies. We do not buy reviews or promise guaranteed removal. See Remove Negative Reviews.

Do I need a website?+

Not always on day one — but a website gives ads and organic traffic somewhere to convert. If you do not have one, or yours does not work on mobile, see web development.

Do you need Local SEO if I already have a Google Business Profile?+

Yes. A Google Business Profile and Local SEO support different parts of local visibility. We can review your business information, services, local search signals, website connection, and overall presence to identify what needs improvement. See Local SEO.

Who pays for Google Ads?+

You pay Google for media. Townloc builds and runs the campaigns. We do not mark up a mystery ad budget. The invoice from Google is yours to see.

Do you buy Google reviews?+

No. We never buy stars, farm accounts, or post fake check-ins. Review work is a timed ask after a real job so customers who already used you can write what they experienced.

Who owns my website?+

You do. Website source files are yours. Cloudflare hosting and SSL are set up at no extra charge on web projects. You pay only for the domain.

Do I keep my Google account?+

Yes. The Business Profile stays in an account in your name. We work inside your access — not a locked dashboard you cannot leave with.
