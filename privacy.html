Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

At TownLoc, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you when you visit townloc.com or contact us about our local digital marketing and website services.

By using our website or submitting a service inquiry, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Who We Are

TownLoc is a local digital marketing and website services practice helping small businesses improve their online presence, local search visibility, customer trust, paid search performance, and website experience.

Our services may include:

  • Local SEO
  • Google Business Profile optimization and setup
  • Google Reviews and reputation management
  • Google Ads campaign management
  • Website design and development
  • Website hosting and technical setup

For privacy-related questions, you can contact us at hello@townloc.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us or submit a service inquiry through our website.

Depending on the service you request, this may include:

  • Your name
  • Business or company name
  • Business email address
  • Phone number
  • Public Google Maps or Google Business Profile URL
  • Service you are interested in
  • Information about your business or project
  • Details you include in your message or inquiry

You can browse most of our website without creating an account.

We do not operate an online checkout or collect payment card information through the website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to operate our business and respond to legitimate service inquiries.

This may include:

  • Responding to your inquiry
  • Understanding your business and project requirements
  • Preparing proposals, estimates, or service recommendations
  • Discussing project scope, timing, and next steps
  • Providing requested local SEO, Google Business Profile, Google Reviews, Google Ads, or website services
  • Communicating with you about an active project or service inquiry
  • Maintaining reasonable business records
  • Protecting our website and services against spam, abuse, and unauthorized activity

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers, review sellers, or unrelated marketing lists.

Google Business Profile and Google Maps Information

If you provide a public Google Maps URL or request assistance with your Google Business Profile, we may review publicly available information about your business.

This information may include:

  • Business name
  • Business location or service area
  • Business categories
  • Business hours
  • Services
  • Photos
  • Customer reviews
  • Public business contact information
  • Other information publicly displayed on Google

We use this information only as reasonably necessary to assess, plan, or provide the requested service.

Your Google Business Profile remains under your ownership and control. We do not claim ownership of your Google account or business listing.

If you choose to provide TownLoc with access to your Business Profile for management or optimization, that access should be granted through the appropriate Google account permissions. You can remove our access at any time.

Google Ads Information

If you request Google Ads services, we may work within a Google Ads account owned or controlled by you.

Depending on the project, we may access information necessary to create, review, optimize, or manage campaigns that you have authorized us to work on.

Google remains responsible for its own platform, billing, and data processing.

Google Ads advertising costs are billed by Google to your account. TownLoc does not require you to provide your payment card information through our website for Google advertising spend.

We do not use your Google Ads account for purposes unrelated to the services you have requested.

Website Development, Hosting, and Technical Access

For website design and development projects, we may need limited access to services that you own or control.

Depending on your project, this may include:

  • Domain registrar
  • Cloudflare
  • Website hosting
  • Content management systems
  • Website administration
  • DNS settings
  • Website deployment services

We request access only when it is reasonably necessary to perform the agreed work.

Whenever possible, clients should maintain ownership of their domain, hosting, accounts, website, source code, and other business assets.

After project completion, access and source files are provided according to the agreed project scope.

Cookies and Website Technologies

TownLoc is designed to use only the website technologies necessary to operate the site and provide its services.

We do not intentionally use invasive tracking technologies or sell individual browsing behavior to third parties.

Your browser may automatically store standard technical information such as cached website resources, device information, or basic connection information required for normal website operation.

If we introduce analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, or other tracking technologies that materially change how personal information is collected or used, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

Service Providers and Third Parties

We may use trusted third-party services when they are necessary to operate our website, process inquiries, communicate with clients, or deliver services.

Depending on the service, these providers may include infrastructure, hosting, email, security, website deployment, analytics, or advertising platforms.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Where access to a third-party account is required for your project, we use that access only for the authorized business purpose.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information submitted through our website and during client projects.

These measures may include:

  • Server-side validation
  • Spam and abuse protection
  • Access controls
  • Authentication for administrative systems
  • Secure communication where supported
  • Limiting access to information to what is reasonably necessary

However, no website, online service, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

For example, inquiry information may be retained while we:

  • Respond to your request
  • Prepare a proposal
  • Discuss a potential project
  • Deliver an agreed service
  • Maintain reasonable business and legal records

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete or securely dispose of it, subject to legitimate business, legal, or security requirements.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information
  • Ask how your information is being used
  • Stop receiving non-essential follow-up communications

To request deletion or correction of information submitted through our website, contact us at hello@townloc.com.

For security purposes, we may need to verify that a request is genuinely related to the person whose information is being requested.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses, business owners, and operators.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 through our website.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete the information where appropriate.

International Visitors

TownLoc may be accessed by visitors from different countries.

If you access our website from outside the country where our business operates, you understand that information you voluntarily submit may be processed through service providers or infrastructure located in other jurisdictions.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information regardless of where it is processed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website, technology, or information practices change.

The Last updated date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

We encourage you to review this page periodically for changes.
