The Problem

Paying for clicks isn't the same as generating customers.

A campaign can spend money and still fail to create useful business opportunities. Broad targeting, irrelevant searches, weak landing pages, poor tracking, or unclear campaign structure can make it difficult to understand where your budget is going.

01

Too Broad

Your ads may appear for searches that don't closely match the service you actually provide.

02

Weak Customer Journey

A customer clicks an ad but lands on a page that doesn't clearly explain the service or next step.

03

Poor Visibility Into Results

If calls, forms, bookings, or other customer actions aren't tracked properly, it's difficult to know what your advertising is actually accomplishing.

The goal isn't more clicks. It's a better path from search to customer.

Google Ads questions, answered.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?+

There isn't one universal budget. A reasonable budget depends on your location, competition, services, customer value, search demand, and business goals. We can help assess what makes sense for your situation.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?+

No. Advertising performance depends on many factors, including demand, competition, budget, targeting, landing pages, tracking, and the strength of the offer. We focus on building and improving the campaign rather than promising a fixed result.

Do I need a website to run Google Ads?+

Not always, but a strong landing page or website can be important for turning relevant clicks into enquiries or customers. We'll assess the customer journey before recommending the campaign structure.

Can you run ads for any business?+

We can work with many local businesses, but suitability depends on the business, market, advertising policies, service, location, and customer demand.

Do I keep access to my Google Ads account?+

Yes. We believe your business should retain access to its advertising infrastructure.

Do you manage campaigns after launch?+

Yes. Campaign management can include monitoring, search-term analysis, conversion review, testing, and ongoing optimization depending on your service arrangement.

Can you guarantee the top position on Google?+

No. No legitimate provider can guarantee a specific ad position or ranking. Ad placement depends on factors such as competition, bid, ad quality, relevance, and auction conditions.

What do you track?+

Depending on your setup, we can track meaningful actions such as calls, forms, bookings, and other website actions. The exact setup depends on your website and customer journey.

Can you help if I already have Google Ads running?+

Yes. We can review an existing campaign and identify structural, targeting, tracking, landing-page, or optimization opportunities.
