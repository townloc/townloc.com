Google Business Profile questions, answered.

What is a Google Business Profile?+

A Google Business Profile is the business listing that can appear when customers search for a business or service on Google. It can provide important information such as your business name, category, hours, location or service area, website, and customer actions.

Do I need an existing Google Business Profile?+

No. We can help with a new setup or optimize an existing profile.

Can you optimize my existing profile?+

Yes. We can review the existing profile and identify the most important areas to improve.

Do you create or buy Google reviews?+

No. We do not create or purchase fake reviews. Our review services focus on legitimate ways to make it easier for real customers to provide feedback. See Google Business Reviews.

Can you guarantee higher Google rankings?+

No. Google rankings depend on many factors and no legitimate provider can guarantee a specific ranking. We focus on proper profile optimization and sound local marketing practices.

Do I keep ownership of my Google Business Profile?+

Yes. Your business should retain control of its Google Business Profile and related accounts.

How long does the setup take?+

The timeline depends on whether you're creating a new profile or optimizing an existing one, as well as the information and access available.
