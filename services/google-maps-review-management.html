Google Business Reviews questions, answered.

How can I get more Google reviews?+

The safest approach is to make it easy for genuine customers to leave feedback after a real experience. This can include sharing your Google review link or QR code through appropriate customer touchpoints.

Can you guarantee a certain number of reviews?+

No. We don't guarantee a specific number of reviews because reviews come from real customers and their genuine experiences.

Do you buy Google reviews?+

No. We never purchase or create fake reviews.

Can I offer customers a discount for leaving a review?+

Google prohibits offering incentives such as discounts or free goods or services in exchange for reviews, review changes, or removal of negative reviews.

Can you help with negative reviews?+

Yes. We can help establish a professional response process and identify reviews that may violate Google's policies and could potentially be reported.

Should I respond to positive reviews?+

Yes. Thoughtful responses can show customers that you value their feedback. Google recommends professional, relevant responses rather than repetitive or overly promotional replies.

Can you remove negative reviews?+

We cannot guarantee removal. If a review appears to violate Google's policies, it can be reported through the appropriate process. Legitimate negative reviews should generally be handled professionally rather than manipulated or suppressed.

Is this service only for home-service businesses?+

No. Our Google Business Reviews service is designed for businesses across many industries, including restaurants, automotive businesses, salons, retail, professional services, repair companies, and other customer-facing businesses.
