They stack. They do not replace each other.

A profile with no reviewsGoogle Business Profile setup puts the pin on the map. Google Reviews fills the stars. They are different jobs.

Reviews without a siteMaps can still send calls. A website makes those clicks convert when someone wants more than a listing.

Ads without a listingYou can buy the click. The map pack still has to look open and safe enough to dial. Empty or unclaimed profiles kill expensive traffic.
