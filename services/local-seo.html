Local SEO questions, answered.

What is Local SEO?+

Local SEO is the practice of improving how your business appears in local search results and Google Maps when nearby customers look for services like yours.

Is Local SEO only about Google Business Profile?+

No. Your profile is a major piece, but Local SEO also covers relevance, consistency, customer signals, and how your broader local presence supports discoverability. See also Google Business Profile Setup.

Can you improve my visibility on Google Maps?+

We focus on legitimate improvements that help customers find and understand your business in Maps and local search. Results vary based on competition, location, and existing presence.

Does Local SEO include website optimization?+

Local SEO and website optimization often work together. We review how your website supports your services, location, and local search intent, and recommend improvements where they can strengthen the overall customer journey.

Can you guarantee higher Google rankings?+

No. Local rankings depend on many factors and no legitimate provider can guarantee a specific ranking. We focus on sound Local SEO practices.

Do I keep ownership of my accounts?+

Yes. Your business should retain control of its Google Business Profile and related accounts.

How long does Local SEO take?+

Timelines vary based on your current local presence, competition, available information, and how much needs to be corrected or improved.
