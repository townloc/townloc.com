Remove Negative Reviews questions, answered.

Can you remove negative Google reviews?+

We cannot guarantee removal. If a review appears to violate Google's policies, it can be reported through the appropriate process. Legitimate negative reviews should generally be handled professionally rather than manipulated or suppressed.

What kinds of reviews can potentially be reported?+

Reviews that appear to violate Google's policies — such as spam, fake content, conflicts of interest, or other guideline issues — may be candidates for reporting. Final decisions are made by Google.

Do you guarantee that unfair reviews will be taken down?+

No. No legitimate provider can guarantee that Google will remove a review. We use a careful, policy-compliant process and set realistic expectations.

Do you offer incentives to get reviews removed?+

No. Google prohibits offering incentives in exchange for review changes or removal of negative reviews.

What if a negative review is legitimate?+

Legitimate feedback should generally be handled with a professional response and, where appropriate, private follow-up. We help you respond carefully without making false claims or pressuring customers.

Is this the same as buying fake positive reviews?+

No. We never purchase, write, or fabricate reviews. This service focuses on careful assessment, response guidance, and policy-based reporting where appropriate. See also Google Business Reviews.

How long does the process take?+

Timelines vary. Assessment and response planning can begin quickly, while any Google review of a reported listing depends on Google's own processes.

Is this service only for home-service businesses?+

No. It is designed for businesses across many industries that rely on Google reputation, including restaurants, automotive businesses, salons, retail, professional services, repair companies, and other customer-facing businesses.
